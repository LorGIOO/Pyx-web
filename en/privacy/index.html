Privacy policyWebsite and application

Your documents are yours.

Neither this website nor Pyx asks you for an account, sets cookies or sends usage statistics. Here, point by point, is the little that leaves your computer and why.

Last updated: 22 November 2026

  • This website uses no cookies, analytics or third-party resources: fonts and images are served from here. It only remembers the theme (light or dark) in your browser if you change it.
  • What you type or paste in the demos stays in your browser. It is not sent anywhere.
  • The Pyx application has no telemetry, automatic error reports or accounts. Your documents do not leave your computer.
  • Pyx connects to the Internet only to look for updates, set up Python or LaTeX when you ask, install a missing library and, if you configure it, check the text with LanguageTool.

1. Who is behind it

Pyx is an MIT-licensed free software project maintained by LorGIOO on GitHub. For any question about this policy, open an issue at github.com/LorGIOO/Pyx/issues. Bear in mind that GitHub issues are public: do not write personal data in them that you do not want published.

2. This website

Hosting

The website is published with GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. As with any page, when you visit it your browser connects to GitHub’s servers and sends them your IP address and technical data of the request (the page you ask for, the browser you use). GitHub may log them for security and operation, according to its privacy statement. Whoever maintains Pyx receives neither those logs nor visit statistics.

Cookies and storage

The website uses no cookies. If you press the theme button, it saves the word light or dark in your browser’s local storage, under the key pyx-tema, to remember it on your next visit. It cannot identify you and it does not leave your browser. You can delete it at any time by clearing this site’s data in your browser.

Third parties

Loading the website contacts no other service: there is no analytics, advertising, embedded video or external fonts. The download, source code and issue links lead to github.com, which has its own policy.

The demos

The lab, the spell checker, completion, table pasting and annotations run entirely in your browser, with JavaScript. What you type, paste or draw is neither saved nor sent, and it disappears when you close the page.

3. The Pyx application

Pyx works offline. It asks for no registration, sends no usage statistics or error reports, and neither reads nor uploads your documents. What you write, your data and your results stay in your project folders and in Pyx’s data folder on your computer: %LOCALAPPDATA%\com.pyx.studio on Windows and ~/.local/share/pyx on Linux. The settings are kept in %APPDATA%\Pyx\config.json (~/.config/Pyx on Linux). When you uninstall Pyx, the uninstaller offers to delete the data folder.

Pyx’s spell checker (red, blue and green) works offline, with dictionaries included in the application. Reading Excel workbooks with \hoja and \celda happens on your computer too. Since Pyx’s code is open, anyone can check this list in the source code.

4. Your rights

Whoever maintains Pyx neither collects nor keeps personal data of the people who visit the website or use the application, so there is no data of yours to consult, correct or delete. About the technical data GitHub logs when serving the website or the downloads, you can exercise your rights with GitHub, as its privacy statement explains. If you believe something does not comply with data protection law, you can also turn to the supervisory authority of your country (in Spain, the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos).

5. Changes to this policy

If a version of Pyx or of the website changes any of the above (for instance, a new connection), this page will be updated first and its release notes will say so. The date above is the last revision.
